# Child safety and AI policy

Version 2026-11-24. This text hasn't been reviewed by a lawyer yet.

## What AI does in Lifestory

- **Checks each photo:** a moderation model refuses sexual, violent or self-harm content, and a vision model checks
  there's one clear face in the photo. It describes what it sees; it doesn't identify anyone.
- **Draws each person:** an image model draws a character sheet of each person in the book's style, from their photo,
  then draws them on each page from that sheet. Pages are never drawn from the photo.
- People write and illustrate the stories. AI only draws your family into them.

## What AI receives

Each photo goes to OpenAI twice, for the check and the sheet. An extra photo also goes once more with the first photo,
to check they show the same person (nobody is identified). {{openai-retention}}
Nothing else about your family goes with it beyond the age you chose and the look you confirmed (hair, eyes, skin tone, freckles, glasses).

## Human review

A person on our team reviews every book before it's printed and approves it or refuses it, saying why. They never
change your book. They see the
character sheets and pages, and your photos only when you allowed it.

## Reporting a problem

If a book or a drawing worries you, reply to any of our emails or write to the address they come from. We read every
report.

## Abusive uploads

We refuse photos that break our terms. When an upload suggests a child is at risk, we close the account and report it
to the authorities where the law requires it.
