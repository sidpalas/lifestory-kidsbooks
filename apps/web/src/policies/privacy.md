# Privacy policy

Version 2026-11-24. For adults 18 and older. This text hasn't been reviewed by a lawyer yet.

## What we collect, and why

- **Your account:** your name and email, to sign you in and email you about your books.
- **Your family's books:** who plays each role (a name, what the children call them, pronouns and an age), your text
  changes, your notes on the drawings, and your proofs.
- **Photos:** a photo of each person in a book, and up to three more of them if you add them, only to draw them.
- **Consent:** what you agreed to at the photo step and when, kept as long as your account.

We don't keep a face template or any biometric identifier, and we don't use your photos to train any model.

## How long we keep it

- A photo that our check refuses is deleted at once.
- Photos are deleted when you place your order with "Make my book", unless you let our team see them during review.
  Then they're deleted when we approve or refuse the book, when you withdraw that permission, or 30 days after the
  order at the latest.
- A book you start and never order is deleted with its photos after 24 hours.
- Character sheets and pages are kept with the book, and deleted when you delete it.
- An unapproved proof is deleted after 30 days.
- You can ask us to delete your account and everything in it.

## Who sees it

- Your family: the members of your family on Lifestory.
- Our team, which reviews every book before it's printed: the character sheets, the pages and your notes. Your
  photos only when you allowed it at the photo step.
- Our processors:
  - **OpenAI** checks each photo and draws the character sheets and pages. Each photo is sent to OpenAI twice (the
    check and the sheet) and never for pages. {{openai-retention}}
  - **Resend** sends our emails.
  - **Railway** hosts the app, its database and its file storage, encrypted in transit and at rest.

## Contact

Write to us at the address our emails come from, to ask what we hold or to have it deleted.
