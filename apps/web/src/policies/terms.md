# Terms of service

Version 2026-11-24. These terms are for adults 18 and older. Lifestory is invite-only while we test it with
friends and family. This text hasn't been reviewed by a lawyer yet, and we'll ask you to agree again when it changes.

## What Lifestory does

Lifestory puts your family into picture books written and illustrated by people. You upload one photo of each person
who plays a role. We check each photo, draw a character sheet of each person in the book's style, and draw them on
every page. A person on our team reviews every book before it's printed.

## Your photos

- You confirm you have the right to use each photo you upload: you're the parent or guardian of any child in it, and
  any adult in it agreed.
- Don't upload photos of other people without their permission, of public figures, or anything sexual, violent or
  harmful.
- We may refuse a photo or a book, for example when a photo breaks these rules or our check can't use it.

## Ordering

- "Make my book" places your order. After it we draw every page, and you can change the drawing on up to 3 pages.
- We review the book and either approve it for print or tell you why we can't print it.
- While Lifestory is invite-only, we settle the price with you directly; nothing is charged in the app.

## The stories

The stories, text and illustrations belong to their authors and illustrators. Your book is for your family's
personal use.
